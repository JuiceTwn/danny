Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-31

Danny is a personal assistant run by [LEGAL NAME OR BUSINESS ENTITY] ("we", "us"). This explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and what control you have. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and, where it applies, Ontario law.

What we collect

Your account
Name, email address and a salted, hashed password.
Waitlist
If you join the list, your email address and the time you did.
Conversations
What you and Danny say to each other. Stored encrypted.
Memory
Facts and preferences Danny remembers about you. You can see them and delete them.
Tasks, goals and reminders
What you ask Danny to track or remind you about.
Approvals and activity log
A record of what Danny asked to do, what you approved, and what ran.
Usage and cost
How much of the service you used, so we can bill and cap it.
Notification details
If you turn on push notifications, the address your browser or phone gives us to reach you.

We don't sell your information. We don't use your conversations, memory or connected-account content to advertise to you.

Connections

Danny only reaches a service after you connect it, and you can disconnect it at any time. Disconnecting deletes the stored access.

  • Gmail (Google). Read your mail and, only after you approve each one, send messages from your account. Access: gmail.readonly, gmail.send. Search terms and message requests go to Google. Email content Danny reads is passed to our AI provider to answer you. Disconnect in Danny's Connections screen, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Google Calendar. Read your events and add or change events after you approve. Access: calendar.events. Event requests go to Google. Event details Danny reads are passed to our AI provider to answer you. Disconnect in Danny's Connections screen, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • GitHub. Read only: review requests, assigned issues and your open pull requests. Access: A personal access token you create and can revoke at any time.. Requests go to GitHub. Titles and status Danny reads are passed to our AI provider to answer you. Disconnect in Danny's Connections screen, or delete the token in your GitHub settings.

Danny asks before it sends a message, spends money or changes something. You approve each action.

Google user data

Danny's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Gmail and Calendar data only to provide the features you asked for. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and people at [LEGAL NAME OR BUSINESS ENTITY] don't read it except with your permission, to investigate abuse, or where the law requires.

How we protect it

Conversations, memory, tokens and similar content are encrypted in our database. Passwords are hashed. Each person's data is separated from everyone else's. Servers are at [HOSTING PROVIDER AND REGION]. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of serious harm to you, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete any memory yourself at any time and it is removed, not hidden. If you ask us to close your account, we delete your data, except what we must keep by law or for backups, which expire on a rolling schedule. Waitlist emails are kept until you ask us to remove them or we've invited you.
