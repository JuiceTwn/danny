The list.

What he's being built to take off your plate.

  • Bills and lettersHe'll read it from a photo.Snap a bill or a letter. He pulls out the amount and the due date, sets the reminder, and has it ready for you to pay.
  • SubscriptionsHe'll find what you forgot you pay for.He goes through your mail and statements, flags what you don't use, and drafts the cancellation for your tap.
  • BookingsHe'll ring to book it.The dentist, the table, the plumber. He makes the call, works around your calendar, and shows you what he'll say first.
  • MailHe'll read it. You'll send it.He drafts the reply and waits on a card showing exactly who gets what. Nothing goes out without your tap.
  • Forwarded mailForward it to Danny.A booking, a school notice, a receipt. He files it, finds the date, and puts it on your calendar.
  • FormsHe'll fill it in.The school form, the insurance claim. He fills what he already knows and asks you only for the rest.
  • TripsTell him where.He lines up flights, a hotel and an itinerary on one card, ready for your tap.
  • ErrandsHe'll build the cart.Groceries, the gift, the refill. He fills the basket. You tap to buy.
  • Your homeLights, heat, locks.Say "heading to bed" and the house turns down. Anything that opens a door asks you first.
  • RepeatsBins, rent, renewals, birthdays.Tell him once. He reminds you every time, a week ahead if you want it, and comes back until it's done.
  • Your dayMorning rundown, evening wrap-up.Weather, calendar, what's open, what's moved. At night: what got done and the top three for tomorrow.
  • HuntsA job, a car, a flat.He watches the web for you and only speaks up when something worth your time shows.
  • ListsGroceries, packing, questions for the doctor.Say it, he adds it. Shared with whoever you live with.
  • TalkType it, say it, or call him.He answers out loud in his own voice. Talk over him and he stops.
  • Your weekA Sunday recap.What he handled, what's still open, what's coming, and what he saved you.
